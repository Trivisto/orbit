Features

Everything you need for the perfect edit sound

Drop in a file, pick a vibe, export. Everything runs on your PC — nothing gets uploaded.

Immersive Space

Eight virtual speakers around and above your head. The sound fills the room instead of sitting in it.

Classic 8D

The sound circling your head — with circle, figure-8, swing and wander paths, and full control over the speed of each lap.

Exact speed

Type any speed down to 0.001×. Slowed style for that deep, moody pitch — or keep the pitch and change only the tempo.

Cinematic reverb

Room, Hall, Arena and Dream reverbs with size, pre-delay, tone and low cut. Slowed + reverb in one click.

Studio-quality export

24-bit WAV or 320 kbps MP3, normalized and ready to drop into CapCut, Premiere, After Effects or any editor.

100% offline

No account, no uploads, no limits. Works with MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A — even the audio from MP4 videos.

Install

Up and running in a minute

Three steps. No account needed.

Download

Click Download for Windows above. You'll get Orbit-Setup.exe.

Run the installer

Open it and click through the setup — pick a folder or keep the default. Orbit opens when it's done.

Put headphones on

Drop any sound in, pick a preset like Immersive or Slowed + Reverb, and hit Export.

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info then Run anyway. That appears for new apps from independent developers that aren't code-signed yet.
FAQ

Questions

Is Orbit free?

Yes — completely free, with no ads, account or watermark.

Do I need headphones?

For the full effect, yes. Immersive and 8D audio rely on each ear hearing something slightly different, which only works properly in headphones or earbuds.

What's the difference between Immersive and 8D?

8D moves the sound in a circle around your head. Immersive makes the sound come from everywhere at once, like you're standing inside it. You can switch between them with one click.

Does it work on Mac or phone?

Right now Orbit is Windows 10 and 11 only.

Can I use the songs I make in my edits?

Orbit changes how a sound feels, not who owns it. Platforms can still recognise copyrighted songs after effects, so only post audio you have the right to use.
